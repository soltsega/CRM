# Product Description: Mini CRM – Client Lead Management System

## Overview
The Mini CRM is a streamlined Client Lead Management System designed for agencies, freelancers, and startups to efficiently manage incoming leads from website contact forms. It provides a secure, user-friendly platform for business owners and admins to track, update, and convert leads into clients, mirroring real-world business workflows.

## Key Features
- **Lead Capture & Storage:** Automatically receive and store lead information (name, email, source) from website contact forms.
- **Lead Status Tracking:** Easily update and monitor the status of each lead as it progresses through the sales funnel: New → Contacted → Converted.
- **Follow-Up Notes:** Add and view notes or follow-up actions for each lead, ensuring no opportunity is missed.
- **Admin Dashboard:** A secure, login-protected dashboard for admins to view, manage, and update all leads.
- **Data Security:** Only authorized users (admins) can access and manage lead data.
- **Database Integration:** All lead details, statuses, and notes are stored in a robust database (MongoDB or MySQL).

## Optional Enhancements
- **Search & Filter:** Quickly find leads using search and filter options.
- **Timestamps:** Track when leads are added, updated, or converted.
- **Analytics:** View simple metrics such as total leads, conversion rates, and follow-up statistics.

## Technology Stack
- **Frontend:** React.js (recommended), HTML, CSS, JavaScript
- **Backend:** Node.js, Express.js
- **Database:** MongoDB (preferred) or MySQL

## User Benefits
- **Efficiency:** Instantly view and manage all incoming leads in one place.
- **Organization:** Track the status and history of every lead, including follow-ups and notes.
- **Security:** Ensure sensitive client data is only accessible to authorized personnel.
- **Scalability:** Built with modern technologies, making it easy to extend with new features.

## Real-World Use Case
A business owner logs into the admin dashboard, sees new leads from the website, updates their status after making contact, adds notes about conversations, and tracks which leads have been successfully converted into clients.
